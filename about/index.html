Why we built Meditize

Simplicity, privacy and science — the three things every break should respect.

In a world that lives behind screens, eye health has become an afterthought. We observed that most productivity tools focus on getting more done, but none focus on how you feel while doing it.

Meditize was born from a simple need: to transform mandatory work breaks into a restorative sanctuary. We didn’t want another annoying pop-up. We wanted a companion that guides you through eye care using the power of sound and movement.

Our Core Principles

  • Simplicity: One click to install, one click to relax.
  • Privacy First: We don’t track your browsing history. Your health is your business.
  • Science-Backed: Every exercise is designed to combat digital eye strain and refresh your vision.
